# StockSentry User Guide (4-Tier Roles)

## Super Admin (Central Headquarters)
1. **Receive Vendor Stock**: Record incoming central inventory under *Transfers & Logistics → Receive Vendor Stock*.
2. **Dispatch City Orders**: Review approved city requests in *Global Requests Queue*, assign carrier/tracking, and dispatch.
3. **Assign Stock to Field**: Use *Assign Stock to Field* to assign kits or bulk-distribute supplies across regions.
4. **Governance**: Manage users, Excel bulk onboarding, alert telemetry, and immutable audit logs.

## Regional Admin
1. **Approve City Replenishment**: Review pending city hub requests in *Stock Approval Queue → Pending Approvals*. Approving reserves central stock.
2. **Review Field Tech Requests**: Approve or *Fulfill & Assign* requests from technicians in your region.
3. **Assign Stock to Field**: Distribute machines or bulk gear to technicians in any city hub in your region.
4. **Repairs & Scrap**: Approve lab repair routing and decommission/scrap requests.

## City Hub Manager
1. **Order Stock**: Use *Stock Refill Store* to request inventory from Central Warehouse, and confirm delivery when shipments arrive.
2. **Assign Stock to Field**: Open *Assign Stock to Field* to assign modems (auto-pick or specific serial), T-shirts (by size), or bulk batches (e.g., 50 T-shirts) to your city's technicians.
3. **Field Tech Requests**: Approve or fulfill gear/machine requests from your technicians.
4. **Offboarding & NOC**: Recover all assigned tools before issuing a clearance NOC.

## Field Technician
1. **Daily Field Jobs & Wi-Fi Setup**: View assigned work orders and complete subscriber ONT activations.
2. **My Assigned Toolbag**: View serialized machines and assigned uniforms/consumable gear.
3. **Request Gear & Supplies**: Browse the gear portal, pick your T-shirt size or machine model, add to your request basket, and send to both your City Manager and Regional Admin.
4. **Report Tool Defect**: Log fault complaints on damaged hardware directly from your toolbag.
